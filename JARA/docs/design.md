## Brand & Style

This design system embodies refined minimalism, architectural restraint, and effortless clarity. Built for modern software platforms requiring cognitive calm and deep focus, it eliminates extraneous ornamentation in favor of purposeful whitespace, crisp structural delineation, and typographic precision.

### Personality & Values
- **Atmospheric & Airy:** Interfaces breathe. Space is not passive emptiness; it is an active structural element that organizes information hierarchy and lowers cognitive load.
- **Quiet Authority:** Premium restraint replaces visual volume. High contrast, precise hairline strokes, and grounded slate anchors project confidence and engineering excellence.
- **Tactile Fluidity:** Every micro-interaction feels weighted, intentional, and responsive without being theatrical.

### Design Movement
Modern Architectural Minimalism with subtle physical cues. Structural hierarchy is formed primarily through structural hairline borders, pure-toned layer transitions, and micro-elevation rather than heavy drop shadows or decorative gradients.

## Colors

The color palette is calibrated around an airy, high-contrast monochrome foundation, energized by an obsidian primary anchor and focused indigo signals for interactive states.

### Palette Roles
- **Canvas Base (`#FAFAFA`):** An ultra-clean, neutral off-white surface that reduces screen glare while establishing an expansive, airy foundation.
- **Surface Layer (`#FFFFFF`):** High-clarity raised card surfaces, modals, and focal containers that float effortlessly against the canvas.
- **Recessed Containers (`#F4F4F5`, `#E4E4E7`):** Tonal fills used for subtle sectioning, code blocks, input interiors, and nested interactive elements.
- **Hairline Dividers (`#E2E8F0`, `#EEEEEE`):** Crisp 1px strokes that introduce structural architecture without visual weight.
- **Primary Accent (`#0F172A`):** Deep obsidian slate providing definitive contrast for principal actions, badges, and high-impact indicators.
- **Interactive Indigo (`#4F46E5`):** Purposeful, restrained accent utilized strictly for focused states, active toggles, subtle link highlights, and critical progression cues.
- **Typography Tiers:**
  - **Primary Black (`#09090B`):** Deep carbon black reserved for headers, metrics, and dominant semantic labels.
  - **Secondary Neutral (`#71717A`):** Balanced zinc tone for body descriptions, helper text, and metadata.
  - **Subtle Muted (`#A1A1AA`):** Inactive iconography, placeholder text, and structural breadcrumbs.

## Typography

Typography establishes an intentional visual cadence. Display and headline levels rely on **Plus Jakarta Sans** for modern architectural poise, subtle geometric character, and tight negative tracking. Core running text, table cells, controls, and administrative data utilize **Inter** to ensure maximum legibility, neutral vertical proportions, and robust multi-weight rendering.

### Principles
- **Generous Leading:** Line heights are calibrated between 1.5x and 1.62x on body levels to enhance scanning comfort across dense dashboards and long-form panels.
- **Optical Tracking:** Larger heading sizes employ deliberate negative letter-spacing (`-0.03em` down to `-0.015em`) to hold letterforms cohesive. Small functional labels (`label-sm`) expand slightly (`0.04em`) to maintain legibility when uppercase styling is applied.
- **Weight Restraint:** Strict limitation to Regular (400), Medium (500), SemiBold (600), and bold accents (700). Avoid intermediate faux-weights to preserve clean visual vertical rhythms.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is grounded in a 4px baseline unit and an 8px grid system. Visual breathing room is treated as a priority asset; dense clusters of information must always be balanced by generous peripheral bounds.

### Responsive Breakpoint Architecture
- **Desktop (1280px+):** Max layout width capped at 1440px with a 12-column fluid grid. Columns are separated by 24px (`space-lg`) gutters with outer framing margins of 48px (`space-2xl`).
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8-column layout with 16px (`space-md`) gutters and 24px outer margins. Multi-column cards collapse to 2-column arrays.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with 16px gutters and 20px margins. Layout stacks vertically; sticky navigation anchors to the base or top with compressed vertical gaps.

### Spacing Philosophy
- Group related metadata with tight increments (`space-2xs` to `space-xs`).
- Separate discrete form clusters and component internal regions using `space-md` to `space-lg`.
- Isolate page regions, canvas views, and card headers with deliberate, uncrowded gaps (`space-xl` to `space-3xl`).

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids layered drops, muddy spreads, or saturated atmospheric shadows. Elevation is primarily expressed through structural 1px borders, subtle tone layering, and ultra-diffused micro-shadows.

### Hierarchy Levels
- **Level 0 (Flat Ground):** `#FAFAFA` base canvas with `#F4F4F5` recessed segments. Borderless or delineated by subtle 1px `#E2E8F0` separators.
- **Level 1 (Card & Content Blocks):** Pure white surfaces (`#FFFFFF`) framed by a 1px `#E2E8F0` hairline border. Shadow is either absent (`shadow-none`) or feather-light: `0 1px 2px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Level 2 (Dropdowns, Flyouts & Tooltips):** Floating white surfaces delineated by a 1px `#E2E8F0` border and an ambient micro-shadow: `0 4px 12px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05), 0 2px 4px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals & Command Palettes):** High-priority overlays surrounded by an ambient hairline ring and an ultra-soft suspension shadow: `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`. Modals sit atop a clean tinted veil: `rgba(9, 9, 11, 0.35)` with an optional 2px backdrop blur.

## Shapes

The geometric silhouette balance pairs precise structural lines with softly eased corners, cultivating an engineered, tactile feel without drifting into playful or bubbly forms.

### Corner Radius Standards
- **Outer Cards & Modals:** Standardized on `0.75rem` (12px, `rounded-xl`) to establish distinct panel autonomy and soft enclosure.
- **Form Elements, Menus & Buttons:** Standardized on `0.5rem` (8px, `rounded-lg`) for clean alignment with standard input heights and typographic leading.
- **Chips, Badges & Micro-tags:** Standardized on `0.25rem` (4px, `rounded-sm`) or fully rounded circular pills (`rounded-full`) depending on status taxonomy.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Deep slate background (`#0F172A`), white text (`#FFFFFF`), 8px border radius, 1px `#0F172A` solid border. Hover shifts smoothly to `#1E293B` with a subtle transform `translateY(-0.5px)` over 150ms.
- **Secondary / Outline:** Pure white background (`#FFFFFF`), slate charcoal text (`#09090B`), 1px `#E2E8F0` border. Hover transitions to `#F4F4F5` with border shifting to `#CBD5E1`.
- **Tertiary / Ghost:** Transparent background, slate gray text (`#71717A`), no border. Hover transitions to `#F4F4F5` with text darkens to `#09090B`.
- **Focus Rings:** All buttons show a 2px offset ring in subtle indigo (`#4F46E5`) with 2px transparent clearance (`ring-offset-2`).

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Text Inputs:** Pure white background (`#FFFFFF`), 1px `#E2E8F0` border, 8px radius, 14px text (`#09090B`), placeholder in `#A1A1AA`.
- **Focus State:** Border shifts directly to `#4F46E5` accompanied by a soft 3px box-shadow glow: `0 0 0 3px rgba(79, 70, 229, 0.12)`. Transition timing is strictly 150ms ease.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** 16px square/circle with a 1px `#CBD5E1` border and 4px radius (checkbox). Checked state fills `#0F172A` with a white checkmark or center dot. Checked focus transitions via `#4F46E5` aura.

### Cards & Panels
- **Structure:** White container (`#FFFFFF`), 12px radius, 1px `#E2E8F0` hairline border, and zero ambient shadow by default.
- **Interactive Variants:** When clickable, cards gain a soft ambient hover shadow `0 6px 16px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05)` and border transitions to `#CBD5E1` within 200ms ease. Padding maintains standard increments: 24px (`space-lg`) on desktop, 16px (`space-md`) on mobile.

### Chips & Badges
- **Informational / Neutral:** `#F4F4F5` background, `#71717A` label, 1px `#E4E4E7` border, 4px corner radius or full pill depending on context.
- **Active / Filtered:** `#0F172A` background, `#FFFFFF` text, seamless borderless finish.
- **Accent Indicator:** Tinted `#EEF2FF` background with `#4F46E5` text and 1px `#E0E7FF` border for system highlights.

### Lists & Tables
- **Dividers:** Separated by horizontal 1px `#EEEEEE` or `#F4F4F5` single-line rules.
- **Row States:** Default rows are transparent; hover states employ a subtle `#F8FAFC` tint across 150ms. No vertical column lines. Numeric and financial figures use tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`).
